# Gauge Theory for Gaps

## 1. Gauge Field
$$
A_\mu = (\Phi, \mathbf{A})
$$

## 2. Gauge Transformation
$$
x_i \to e^{i\theta_i} x_i
$$

## 3. Rotation Angle
$$
\theta = \arctan\!\left(\frac{\sum m_i (\mathbf{x}_i \times \mathbf{v}_i)}{\sum m_i (\mathbf{x}_i \cdot \mathbf{v}_i)}\right)
$$

## 4. Angular Momentum Conservation
$$
L = \sum m_i (\mathbf{x}_i \times \mathbf{v}_i) = \text{const}
$$

## 5. Curvature
$$
F_{\mu\nu} = \partial_\mu A_\nu - \partial_\nu A_\mu
$$

## 6. Application to Gaps
In upward gap: $\theta > 0$ → position rotation toward other assets.

## 7. Stability Condition
$$
\frac{\partial^2 \Pi}{\partial \theta^2}\bigg|_{\theta^\star} < 0
$$
